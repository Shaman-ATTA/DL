**Title:** Dragonshard Legends: Tap RPG
**Short description (≤80):** Tap to slay monsters, hire fantasy heroes and restore the shattered Dragon Shard!

The Dragon Shard that kept the realm of Elaria at peace has shattered, and monsters pour out of enchanted forests, crystal caves, lava wastes and frozen peaks. Become the Shardbearer and fight back!

• Tap to attack: every tap deals damage, with crits, hit flashes and showers of gold
• Bosses: dragons, the Frost King and a Lich. Beat them before the timer runs out
• 8 heroes to hire: Lirael the Archer, Borin Ironbeard, Embra Flamecaller, Vex Shadowstep, Sir Aurel, Thornwyn the Druid, Kael Dragonrider and Nyssa Stormweaver. Each one gets big power spikes at milestone levels
• 6 spells: Shard Blade, Berserk Rage, Arcane Haste, Meteor Call, Golden Rain and Time Ward
• Prestige: shatter your progress for Dragon Shards and buy permanent artifacts
• Offline gold: your allies keep fighting while you're away
• Works offline. No ads, no data collection
• English and Russian
